# Feedback from Ballot CH Core STU 7

## General information
The STU 7 ballot ([IG v7.0.0-ballot](https://fhir.ch/ig/ch-core/7.0.0-ballot/index.html)) was held from August 7 to September 30, 2026. The annual Projectathon took place from September 22-23, 2026, with the opportunity to test and provide feedback on the CH Core-based exchange formats and thus also CH Core.

Votes cast: 21 (YES: 20, NO: 0, ABSTAIN: 1). There were no negative votes.

During the ballot, 20 issues were reported for CH Core (see [CH Core](https://github.com/hl7ch/ch-core/issues?q=is%3Aissue%20label%3A%22STU%207%20Ballot%22) and [CH Term](https://github.com/hl7ch/ch-term/issues/181)). In addition, several issues in derived IGs (CH VACD, CH EMR, CH EMS) concern CH Core; they are listed in [Related issues in derived IGs](#related-issues-in-derived-igs). Here is a brief summary.

Effort estimate:
* **Low**: editorial or a single, local change in FSH, with no discussion needed
* **Medium**: profile change that affects examples or derived IGs, or that needs a decision in the working group
* **High**: new design or mapping work, or input from external organizations needed
